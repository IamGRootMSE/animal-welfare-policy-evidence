## Third-party sources

Source publications, excerpts, images, trademarks, and downloaded files remain subject to their own rights and terms. A source URL, successful download, local snapshot, or SHA-256 hash is not a republication license. Government-hosted pages can contain third-party material; the script's `.gov` storage filter is not a legal determination that every byte is public domain.

Peer-reviewed articles are **not presumed to be public domain**. Where an article has an explicit reuse license, consult that license and its attribution requirements. Where it does not, the repository's MIT license does not authorize redistribution of its full text. The source ledger links to publications and records the factual basis of claims without relicensing those publications.

Local snapshots under `data/raw/snapshots/` preserve available source bytes for review and provenance. The website exposes research exports and retrieval metadata; it does not automatically publish every snapshot. Before redistributing source files, check the rights associated with the specific work and its embedded content.
